Tailscale Funnel path has a COLD-START penalty well past that budget:

  HEAD 1 (cold)  : 405 in 6.514s   <- past the 2500ms guard

(proven above not to touch any counted state) made the turn complete on

SCOPE, stated exactly: this is a property of THIS LANE'S public ingress path,
not of the code under change, and the product's behaviour under it is CORRECT

here claims the guard's 2500ms budget is right or wrong for a real deployment.
