carries an `mcp_toolset`.

STILL AN INFERENCE, stated as one: no per-request access log was captured, so
attributing those 6 to Anthropic's hosted-MCP relay rests on (a) the public
origin being the only MCP URL any provider was handed, (b) unauthenticated
traffic provably not counting, and (c) the delta landing exactly on the single
turn that mounted an mcp_toolset. It is not a recorded request identity.
